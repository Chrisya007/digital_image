# Bit Depth

Bit depth determines how many distinct brightness steps one channel can represent. For `n` bits, the source article gives the formula `2^n`. Its examples are 1-bit binary with 2 tones, 8-bit grayscale with 256 shades, and 24-bit true color with three 8-bit RGB channels and 16,777,216 possible colors.
