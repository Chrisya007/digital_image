# CIELAB

CIELAB (`L*a*b*`) is described by the source article as perceptually uniform. `L*` represents lightness from `0` (black) to `100` (diffuse white); `a*` is the green-to-red opponent axis; and `b*` is the blue-to-yellow opponent axis. The article gives `ΔE = √((ΔL*)² + (Δa*)² + (Δb*)²)` as a Euclidean color-difference measure.
