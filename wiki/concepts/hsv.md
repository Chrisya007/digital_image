# HSV

HSV represents color through hue, saturation, and value. In the source article, hue is measured around a circle from `0°` to `360°`, saturation ranges from `0%` (gray) to `100%` (vivid pigment), and value ranges from `0%` (black) to `100%` (full brightness).
