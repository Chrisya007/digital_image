# JPEG

JPEG is described as lossy compression suited to natural photographs. The source article outlines YCbCr conversion and chroma subsampling, DCT of `8 × 8` pixel blocks, and frequency quantization that rounds small coefficients to zero. It notes that over-compressing sharp edges or text can cause ringing and `8 × 8` blocking artifacts.
