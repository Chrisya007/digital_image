# Ordered Dithering

Ordered dithering compares pixel intensity with a fixed, repeating threshold grid called a Bayer matrix before rounding to the nearest palette color. The source article gives `2 × 2` and `4 × 4` matrices as examples and describes the method as fast, with a distinctive crosshatch texture.
