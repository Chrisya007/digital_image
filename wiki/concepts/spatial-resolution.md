# Spatial Resolution

Spatial resolution is the width and height of an image's pixel grid. The source article gives `1920 × 1080` pixels as `2,073,600` total pixels, roughly `2.07` megapixels. It notes that zooming far into a low-resolution grid reveals square blocks called pixelation.
